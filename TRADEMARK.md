# IPXO Trademark and Brand Usage Policy

IPXO names, logos, symbols, and related brand elements are trademarks and/or
trade dress of IPXO. Trademark rights are not granted by source-code or asset
license terms.

## Allowed Without Additional Permission

- Referential use to identify IPXO as a company, product, or service.
- Editorial use (news, reviews, commentary) that does not imply endorsement.
- Linking to official IPXO properties while following this policy.

## Not Allowed

- Modifying logos/symbols (except proportional resizing).
- Using IPXO marks in a way that implies sponsorship, endorsement, or
  partnership where none exists.
- Using IPXO marks in app names, product names, domains, social handles,
  or branding that is confusingly similar.
- Combining IPXO marks with other marks to create a new composite logo.
- Using outdated, low-quality, or unofficial mark files when official files are
  available from this repository.

## Required Attribution Context

When reasonable for the medium, include a statement such as:
"IPXO and related marks are trademarks of IPXO."

## Requests Requiring Written Permission

- Co-branding, partner campaigns, merchandise, paid media, or event signage.
- Any use that departs from this policy or from `BRAND_GUIDELINES.md`.

For special permission requests, open an issue in this repository.
